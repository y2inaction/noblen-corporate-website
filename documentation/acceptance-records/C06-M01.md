## 1. Purpose

Walk through the publication workflow as three different people, one in each role, and judge whether the steps and messages are clear. Automated tests C06-001 and C06-002 exist and do not replace this record.

## 2. Prerequisites

- A staging copy of WordPress 6.4 or newer on PHP 8.3 or newer, built from the release packages (`scripts/build-packages.sh`: `noblen-corporate-theme.zip`, `noblen-corporate-core.zip`, `SHA256SUMS`), never production. WP_DEBUG and WP_DEBUG_LOG on so that PHP problems are recorded.
- Three separate accounts: Noblen Content Editor, Noblen Clearance Officer, Noblen Publication Approver. The audit key is configured in `wp-config.php` (the owner supplies a test key for staging). Separation of duties is on (the default).
