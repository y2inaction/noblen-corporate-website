## 1. Purpose

The owner walks through the custody of the audit key and the recovery procedure and confirms that the people and the copies exist. Key rotation and recovery after loss exist only as developer-run PHP functions today: there is no screen and no command for them. The owner decides whether a developer-assisted rehearsal is needed (step 6).

## 2. Prerequisites
